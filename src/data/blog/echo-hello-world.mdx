I'm Vignesh, a grad student at [NYU](https://nyu.edu) studying Computer Science with a focus on Systems. 

I've put off starting a blog for a long time. This is me finally doing it.

## Why Start a Blog

A few reasons:
- Writing forces real understanding. It's easy to think you know something until you try to explain it.
- Future me will likely be glad this exists.
- It's a way to share what I learn as I learn it, not just after the fact.

## What to Expect

Posts here will mostly cover systems programming and web development, with the occasional detour into whatever I'm working through, LeetCode problems included. There's no fixed schedule and no fixed topic.

## How This Blog Is Built

The stack is `Astro`, `MDX`, and `Tailwind`, hosted on `GitHub Pages`. I picked this stack because it's fast, simple and lets me writing in plain markdown.
